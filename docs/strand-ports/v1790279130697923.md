## 3. Where the export's prose and the compiled bundle disagree (the bundle governs)

1. `MediaBlock.d.ts` still says of `ratio`: "Absent, every kind renders exactly as before." That was
   correction 26's claim. Correction 27 changed the unratioed gallery (declared rows and `minHeight: 0`), and
   27 §1 says the `.d.ts` and the prompt were left unchanged because "neither described the overflow". Read
   against the bundle, the sentence is true of image, video, link and audio and not of a gallery. The port's
   own comment on `ratio` says which kinds are unchanged.
2. `MediaBlock.prompt.md` says "Absent, nothing changes", the same claim, and the same reading applies.
3. Neither the extractions nor the `.d.ts` name a one-image gallery. The bundle gives it one declared row.
   `postCardProps` never builds one (one image is `image`), so no page reaches it.

## 4. What changes with no prop passed

- **A gallery of three or four images** now fills its 16/10 frame. At `ea35e8e` the rows were implicit, sized
  by each image's intrinsic height, so the tiles spilled past the frame and the lower row was clipped (27 §0).
  The frame's own box is unchanged; the tiles are what move. Reached by any non-Convene Feed card with three
  or four images.
- **A gallery of two images** carries `grid-template-rows: minmax(0,1fr)` and `min-height: 0` on each tile;
  every box size is equal to `ea35e8e` (27 §2's measured row).
- **Image, video, link and audio** without `ratio`: unchanged.

With `ratio`, which only the Convene Feed card passes: the media box is 16:9 inside the frame's edge, one
image or several (1077, 1115).
