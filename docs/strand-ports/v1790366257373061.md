## 3. Where the export's prose and the compiled bundle disagree (the bundle governs)

1. The extraction, the handoff and the `.d.ts` write Hide list's tracks as `0 minmax(0,720px)`. The
   bundle writes `0px minmax(0,{hiddenWidth})`, with 720 the default. The layout is the same, and
   the readout itself reads `0px 720px`.
2. `Pane.d.ts` says nothing about tier for the tool handlers. In the bundle the branch sits after the
   route form's return, so the toolbar never renders below expanded.
3. `Pane.d.ts` does not say that `listHidden` needs `paneWidth`, `height` or a handler, or that
   a closed pane ignores it. The bundle's guard and `hideList = open && listHidden` do both.
4. The bundle always wraps the pane's content in `[data-pane-body]` inside the new branch, bounded or
   not; the prose mentions the body only under `height`.
5. The toolbar is named by `title`. A caller that passes none gets an unnamed toolbar. Discovery
   passes the open event's title.
6. `PostCard`'s discovery doc comment in the bundle still carries correction 25's "two lines held"
   and "No going row, no Report" above the correction 28 and 29 paragraphs that supersede them.
   `PostCard.prompt.md` does the same, and `PostCard.d.ts` says "the reason row holding two lines"
   under `presentation` while its `reason` says one line of 28.
7. `PostCard.d.ts` types `title` as a string; the port has typed it as a node since the first port.
8. Extraction item 4 reads the second checklist column at 150.04 and one column at 149.94, and
   calls that a rail of 192.4. The template flips at exactly 150. With this tree's 1px
   `--border-thin` that is a 192 rail; 192.4 assumes the readout browser's 1.2 edge.
9. The extraction and the `.d.ts` say the heading slot is "absent when no facet is set". In the
   bundle only the Button is gated; the empty live-region wrapper is always drawn under `"heading"`.
   As an empty flex item it adds one `--space-2` gap, so the title is 8 narrower than under
   `"foot"`.
10. `MediaBlock.d.ts` and `.prompt.md` still say that without `ratio` every kind renders as before.
    That has been untrue of the gallery since correction 27 (the prior record's section 3), and it
    carries forward. Both files now also say the ratioed image stays inside its edge on WebKit, and
    the extraction says WebKit was not available to the readout.
11. The bundle's `MediaBlock` comment attributes the former overrun to WebKit alone. Extraction
    item 7 read it in Chrome too, by 2.3 to 2.4px (G116's amendment).
12. Every compile comment in this pass cites B9-SPEC by the line numbers of the spec at `d23c55f`
    (14 for the pane, 20 for Filters, 26 and 27 for the card and the Menu). Revision 2, committed in
    this PR, puts them at 19, 28, 42 to 47 and 49. The ports cite the Revision 2 line by its
    subject.
13. The extraction (line 17) and `Pane`'s doc say the hidden list keeps its scroll. The bundle's
    follow (`_ds_bundle.js:3154-3157`) runs on every new `selectedKey` with no `listHidden` check,
    and the hidden list is still laid out at no width, so a step while hidden writes its scroll.
    The port keeps the bundle's effect (844). Discovery holds the key it passes while the list is
    hidden, and G128 owes Strand the guard.
