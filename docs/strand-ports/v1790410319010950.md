## 3. Where the export's prose and the compiled bundle disagree (the bundle governs)

1. `Tooltip.prompt.md` says "No `title=` anywhere in Strand". The bundle still writes `title` in four
   parts: FacetRail's collapsed strip (the expand button and each axis button), LensBar's icon-first
   seat on a pointer, VerbChip's disabled reason, and PostCard's "Written by you" pen icon. It holds
   of IconButton only. The ports keep the four as compiled (G137).
2. The bundle's `Tooltip` comment and the extraction say `input` is taken "as PersonCard and
   BrowseTile take it". `BrowseTile` accepts `input` and reads nothing from it; only `PersonCard`
   uses it, for its hover edge.
3. The Tooltip's prose places it "below the control". The bundle places it against the host span,
   which is the control only while the control is in flow (G136).
4. Correction 31 §5 says `--empty-badge` and `--empty-measure` leave the rewrite with no raw size.
   `EmptyState` still hands `CBadge` `size={48}`, and the badge's glyph (56% of it) and its radius
   (10 at 48 and up) come from that number; the token sizes only the badge's box. At today's value
   the two agree.
5. The extraction and the bundle's header cite the joint ratification as 1152. The handoff, which is
   Chat's own statement of it, says 1153; this record uses 1153.
6. `EmptyState.prompt.md` gives a scrolling page's column `minHeight: calc(100dvh - …)`. In this shell
   a surface's column does not start at the top of the scroller: the lens bar, a filter row or a
   greeting sits above it, so a viewport calc on the column runs past the visible foot. What was
   built is in section 7, item 3.
