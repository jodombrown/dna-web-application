## Search (1124, reopening 661)
One Input, `aria-label` "Search events", placeholder "Search events, hosts, places". Enter applies the text as the `q` facet and shows it as an applied chip; Escape clears the draft. Matches, on the server, a case-insensitive substring of the trimmed text (1 to 100 characters; `%`, `_` and `\` taken literally; longer refused with 22023) against the title, the presenter's name as the event page resolves it, the host's name, the physical place's name, city, region and country, and the family label (1159): `convene_discovery` takes the text and narrows the corpus before lanes form, and lanes re-render over the narrowed corpus. While a search is applied the floors do not apply: a lane holding any match shows and a lane with no match drops (1173). No result count, no suggestion list at launch.

## Lens bar (693, 1093, 1125)
LensBar, `labels="always" icons` at every tier, `width="content"` at every tier: each seat sized to its own label, never stretched across the row (1145); at medium and expanded the bar and the search field centre together as one unit (1170). Lenses from the runtime table: all (All), follow (Communities), taste (For you), curated (Curated), network (My network). A lens says who the events come from; a facet says what the event is. Any lens but All renders that section as a grid of the same card, `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(var(--lane-card-width),1fr))`, gap 12, filling the column (1125). No tile and no second card (578). **More on Convene (1148):** when a lens holds less than one full row at the current width, its own cards render first and, beneath them under the heading "More on Convene", All's lanes with the lens's events removed. The heading is h2 display 22 like a lane's. A lens with no events keeps its EmptyState (724) and shows no More on Convene. A lane emptied by the removal drops; floors are not re-applied after the removal until the second handoff gives the projection an exclusion (1174).

## Lanes (631 as amended by 1092 and 1124; 632, 633)
Base order, from `convene_lanes`: Happening soon, This weekend, Join from anywhere, Filling up, New this week, Curated by Convene, From communities you follow, Because of what you follow, Near your homes, Connected to your network. Filling up is a row in the table, not a literal (1124). The Browse lane is withdrawn at every width (1172): a tile applied exactly the Topics or Place facet its checkbox applies.

Floors (a lane renders only at or above): soon 4, weekend 2, online 2, filling 2, fresh 2, curated 1, follow 1 (one sentence while following none), taste 2, near 2, network 1.

- **Learned order (1124, reversing 1091).** A lane the member acted in during the last seven days (opened a card from it, saved from it, followed from it) moves up, most recent first; the base order holds beneath. Nothing hides. Stored per member in `public.member_lane_activity` (one row per lane, the latest act and its time), written only through `note_lane_act(p_lane, p_act)` from those three acts, never on load and never by the pane (1160). The projection returns sections already in this order and a `lane_order` naming every lane in it; the surface never re-sorts by position. Reordering is not animated.
- **Filling up (1124, reversing 1090).** Events with five or more going as the viewer's own row policy returns them, counted as `event_page` counts them (1157), ranked by RSVP momentum over the last 72 hours (going registrations created in the window); the rank is never shown. The card's last row carries the going names under 1128. No See all.
- **Near your homes** ranks the member's homes in profile order and never filters.
- **See all:** Happening soon applies `when`; relationship lanes switch lens; This weekend (1112), Join from anywhere (1122) and Filling up carry none.
- Each lane: h2 display 22, a row of cards at `--lane-card-width` (320, 1087), gap 12, that scrolls sideways. Dismissals per lane (581) through `dismiss_discovery_item`.
